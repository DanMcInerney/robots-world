# Jev ecosystem techniques for camera-based drone control

19 September 2026. Source research only; no project dependencies, inference, simulation campaigns or physical flights were run.

**My recommendation is to keep the stereo/metric-perception architecture in the [saved camera-follow report](JEV-CAMERA-FOLLOW-RECOMMENDATION.md), then test three specific changes: let Jev request missing evidence, test whether the number of similar action options biases its decisions, and preserve ambiguity throughout target selection.** The most useful ideas came from code search, browser automation and context pruning. They are mechanisms to adapt, not libraries I would install wholesale into the flight loop.

The missing experiment remains integrated camera-derived search, same-car identification, metric following and active recovery. Joint velocity/yaw menus, computed consequences, compact maps, bounded history and hybrid control were already tried or proposed. The [handoff](JEV-CAMERA-FOLLOW-HANDOFF.md) distinguishes the 29%→74% camera-derived yaw-framing result from the earlier 0/24 full-mission confirmation; neither establishes sustained 10 m following.

## The most promising transfers

**1. Make missing evidence an explicit decision.** `jev-code` distinguishes unjudged, judged, failed and parked candidates; asks whether the decisive content is visible or cut off; and can acquire another excerpt. Sentinel similarly lets Jev choose a source of additional context. These are more concrete than merely adding history. [Find workflow](https://github.com/devagrawal09/jev-code/blob/fd092558ebea389c81d44f9b10e826d9a72afaa3/src/workflows/find.ts#L423-L501), [second inspection](https://github.com/devagrawal09/jev-code/blob/fd092558ebea389c81d44f9b10e826d9a72afaa3/src/workflows/find.ts#L637-L708), [Sentinel source choice](https://github.com/harshwasan/pi-jev-sentinel/blob/8a9ae145eaf165b9ff1c600ea0188a585aa12db5/src/guard.ts#L731-L751).

For the drone, offer bounded alternatives such as refreshing synchronized depth, reprocessing an existing target crop, comparing a retained appearance observation, or choosing a maneuver that exposes an occluded area. Perception produces the resulting evidence; Jev chooses the request or viewpoint. A crop cannot reveal a hidden surface, and repeating a judgment over the same detector text adds no independent visual evidence. The next decision must use the resulting observation, its acquisition time and the current goal. This is my highest-priority new policy experiment.

**2. Test whether redundant maneuvers distort the hold/move decision.** Jevpilot separates drive/stop from an explicitly conditional path question, to avoid many similar moving paths splitting preference against one stop option. Code resolves the path only if motion chooses drive. Its simulator geometry, candidate filtering and stop restrictions provide substantial assistance. The related Simple-Jev demo reuses the idea with substitute classifiers; it is not an independent confirmation. Neither source establishes that this factorization improves our drone policy. [Jevpilot request](https://github.com/standardagents/jevpilot/blob/e1beeb13b9a928fb76f167f86af584f4ce9cf180/src/jev-request.js#L298-L360), [resolution and rationale](https://github.com/standardagents/jevpilot/blob/e1beeb13b9a928fb76f167f86af584f4ce9cf180/src/planning.js#L403-L444), [substitute-model adapter](https://github.com/featherless-ai/simple-jev/blob/0dd5396ffce671ab7c4bfc031506d8e558cf8d23/demos/jevpilot/src/simple-jev-api.js#L27-L78).

Keep the physical actions and observation fixed, duplicate equivalent movement options under new IDs, and measure whether Jev changes its physical choice. Compare a flat menu with `hold or move` plus `assuming movement, choose this complete maneuver`. Both questions can inspect the same facts independently; the second does not consume its sibling's answer. Conditional resolution happens in code. Always retain the hold option in this experiment. Conditional batching is familiar; **sensitivity to equivalent candidate counts** is the sharper untried question. Multiplying the two reported distributions does not establish calibrated physical probabilities.

**3. Preserve “none,” “ambiguous” and “not evaluated” through every selection stage.** Tontoko's browser selector inspects all option partitions, allows none/ambiguous within each, and rejects a global result unless exactly one match remains. WrongStack separately asks which offered candidate is best and whether any candidate fits. This matters because “best blue car currently visible” does not imply “the same blue car we were following.” [Complete partition resolution](https://github.com/tontoko/jev-browser/blob/05b8257db8d7df77a82274016de84d965a38214f/src/actions.ts#L70-L102), [relative versus absolute fit](https://github.com/WrongStack/WrongStack/blob/4cf97c0aa4f855751949ee8e99c719d06f4c26e0/packages/core/src/coordination/typesafe-dispatch-classifier.ts#L80-L159).

Keep true-target candidate recall, within-menu matching and actual reacquisition separate. A complete examination of supplied detections is still not complete visual coverage of the world. Preserve deferred viewpoints and distinguish unseen, occluded, inspected without detection, and failed acquisition. Blink's unresolved search allocation is useful bookkeeping, but its branch weights are not target-location probabilities. [Allocation and unresolved branches](https://github.com/ellipsis-dev/blink/blob/a621ede75649303a933828c18c27ad800bb43ef0/src/walkers.ts#L6-L98).

**4. Revalidate what the action depends on, including cached decisions.** A Cline browser integration allows an unchanged click target to survive irrelevant page-text changes, while rechecking target identity, form state, destination and visibility. Skillbox re-reads the entire relevant catalog after inference before delivering or caching a result. This sharpens the already-existing expiry/goal-epoch idea. [Action-relevant freshness](https://github.com/abeatrix/cline-plugin-jev-browser/blob/c886dcb35cb7d64d682a56211f9b04a5345c6433/src/jev-browser.ts#L353-L411), [post-inference revision check](https://github.com/kitze/skillbox/blob/d83ba4ecd254c8dfa6a759d1feb5141384e26a9e/src/server/recommendations.ts#L203-L315).

For a maneuver, recheck goal/target identity, relevant clearance and visibility bounds, observation age, execution ownership and the remaining execution-plus-braking horizon. Ordinary pixel motion should not invalidate every answer; a changed obstacle corridor must. Browser equality checks do not solve continuous physical validity, so the tolerances and prediction errors need qualification. Any locally admitted motion retains a bounded lease; a healthy MAVLink stream is not fresh reasoning.

**5. Preserve evidence without circular deletion.** Yoshi's default memory path uses retained evidence that cannot itself be removed. Its separate snapshot path checks proposed omissions with all proposed removals absent. That prevents “discard A because B covers it” and “discard B because A covers it.” Jev-pruner additionally retains records when not every relevant history segment was evaluated. [Immutable witnesses](https://github.com/compozy/yoshi/blob/55c719718e5039f2276fbad804211682ae012f1e/src/context.ts#L378-L418), [joint omission check](https://github.com/compozy/yoshi/blob/55c719718e5039f2276fbad804211682ae012f1e/src/context.ts#L723-L798), [coverage accounting](https://github.com/tamaratran/jev-pruner/blob/c5bf7df482b9f8df3dcb838034f06da0d8d0d07a/src/output.ts#L309-L390).

Apply that principle only to the delivered history view. Preserve original observations under bounded retention, identity contradictions, unread sightings, the exact active goal and uncertain command IDs. Do not copy Yoshi's default sticky omissions across goal changes: its lifecycle intentionally prioritizes a stable context prefix. Memory is a second-stage experiment; our previous history experiments did not establish a winning treatment.

Its exact simulator state and coded emergency actions are assistance; camera uncertainty must enter our residual bounds. Test preserving an active pursuit job through repeated decisions while continuing required MAVLink setpoint refresh and expiry checks. Its fallback selection and bounded cancellation wait are not a flight-qualified execution contract. [Routine control](https://github.com/Skyvern-AI/jevscape/blob/8fe4d37349fc6a8d03ac1c918302eef254bb56c3/agents/jev/controller.ts#L690-L788). |
| Event-origin deadlines | AIAvatarKit counts its semantic hold from the triggering event, so delayed inference does not start a fresh waiting period. Apply the same accounting to review/expiry clocks. Its conversational error fallback must not become permission to move. [Clock logic](https://github.com/uezo/aiavatarkit/blob/38b617b8b9269939734e70ef503d7ea6976acdbd/aiavatar/sts/vad/turn_end_gates/manager.py#L58-L95). |
| Persistent intention versus transient interruption | LiteLLM excludes temporary failures from durable routing preference and anchors stall detection on the newest action. Retain Jev's target intention through a temporary expired observation; measure repeated lack of progress before rejecting repetition. Repeated velocity setpoints are normal following behavior. [Persistence exclusions](https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm/blob/56116079c8022da0e8f7ff9ccb017ad5aca5aed2/litellm/router_strategy/complexity_router/complexity_router.py#L929-L981), [stall anchoring](https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm/blob/56116079c8022da0e8f7ff9ccb017ad5aca5aed2/litellm/router_strategy/complexity_router/stall_detector.py#L92-L118). |

## What I would test next

Start with a small camera-derived replay set containing the actual car, lookalikes, missing depth, clipped evidence and incomplete candidate lists. Test candidate duplication/order, explicit no-match and evidence-request decisions separately. Report how often the correct target reaches the menu before measuring Jev's choice. These tests locate representation problems; recordings cannot establish control performance.

Then run paired closed-loop episodes on the integrated metric-perception baseline, adding one treatment at a time. Match initial conditions, target trajectories, sensor pipeline, complete physical action set and local safety rules; let images reflect each controller's actual motion. Include a deterministic selector over the same menu to expose how much policy the surrounding code supplies. Use held-out episode families, measure run-to-run variation, and count errors and abstentions rather than discarding them.

Score correct-identity time in a declared 10 m tolerance band, mistaken locks, active recovery, collisions/clearance, source-to-command age and total inference cost. Inject slow replies, changed goals and target-ID reuse separately to check command validity. An observed target reappearing on its own is not evidence of successful active search.

I would not begin by installing large coding frameworks, adding more agents or replacing the actuator selector with a planner while still attributing its decisions to Jev. Several projects silently force a real winner after a none judgment, return an action after cancellation, or cache decisions without their current task context. These are useful counterexamples, not flight-ready defaults. The detailed lane reports preserve those exact cases and their source limits.
