Mechanical qualification only

These runs hold the observer still. The target moves independently through fixed corridors while 320 by 180 RGB images are acquired at 5 Hz with 100 ms delivery latency and 2 percent dropout. The displayed blue detections come from actual rendered pixels. No Jev call or navigation policy runs in this qualification.

All campaign flights give Jev search, target, movement, speed, yaw, pitch and field-of-view choices. Code performs perception, sensor bookkeeping, coordinate conversion and selected-setpoint stabilization. There is no automatic scan, aiming servo, follower or route planner.

The spectator geometry and offline target-facing witness are evaluator evidence only. Clear witness routes establish reachability, not a sensor-only planning method. The recovery course allows passive reappearance as well as physical screen occlusion; a later sighting alone does not establish active recovery. The uniquely blue box does not qualify semantic car recognition.
